Solar Gravitational Lens biosignature spectroscopy

MIT · Research Assistant, Seager group · May 2025 to present · with A. Glidden, S. Barat, S. Turyshev

  • Built a Python spectroscopic modeling pipeline largely from first principles to model high-resolution transmission spectra of Earth-like exoplanets amplified by the Solar Gravitational Lens.
  • Learned the HITRAN API from scratch for high-resolution molecular line data, building from test scripts to full models, and used colorednoise to simulate noise in synthetic observations.
  • Quantified biosignature detectability limits as a function of spatial resolution, spectral resolution, and signal-to-noise, using cross-correlation and log-likelihood analysis.

At JWST-like resolving power (R ~ 10³), overlapping features from different molecules blend together and make trace-gas biosignatures hard to identify. The SGL's photon amplification (~10¹¹ at ~1 μm) can instead be spent on spectroscopy, reaching R ~ 10⁶–10⁷, with practical limits set by the solar-corona background, the SGL point-spread function, and reconstruction losses.

Tools: Python, NumPy, SciPy, HITRAN HAPI, colorednoise

First-author poster, AASTCS 11: Exoplanet Atmospheres (2026). First-author manuscript in preparation.

Presenting the SGL work at AASTCS 11: Exoplanet Atmospheres (2026).

Magma oceans and exoplanet atmospheres

MIT · Research Assistant, Seager group · May 2026 to present

  • Coupling LavAtmos (melt–vapor chemistry), FastChem (gas-phase equilibrium chemistry), and petitRADTRANS (radiative transfer) to connect magma ocean interiors to observable atmospheric spectra.
  • Evaluating atmospheric tracers as diagnostics of exoplanet interiors.

Tools: petitRADTRANS, LavAtmos, FastChem, Python

Contributing author on a JWST Cycle 6 proposal for a young sub-Neptune.

Morning Star Missions to Venus

MIT · Research Assistant · Jan 2025 to Aug 2026 · advisors J. Petkowski, S. Seager

  • Designed a UV phase-modulation experiment, using a lock-in amplifier, to test the feasibility of in-situ photonic measurement of the Venusian atmosphere.
  • Spectroscopically characterized molecules and curated a spectral library of 10 molecule classes relevant to Venus's atmosphere and to the search for organic compounds.
  • Presented findings to collaborators at the University of Bern and mentored two high school students on the spectral library.

Corruption-robust non-negative matrix factorization

Harvey Mudd College · Undergraduate Research Assistant · Aug to Dec 2024 · advisor J. Haddock

  • Developed quantile-based multiplicative update rules for NMF that resist sparse corruption, working with two other undergraduates on the algorithm, simulations, and paper.
  • Analyzed convergence behavior under sparse noise.

Peer-reviewed conference paper, SampTA 2025.
