MIT research · 2025 to present

Solar Gravitational Lens spectroscopy

I built a Python pipeline, largely from first principles, that models high-resolution transmission spectra of Earth-like exoplanets amplified by the Sun's gravitational lens, and used it to find how well biosignatures could be detected at different spatial and spectral resolutions. I presented the work as a first-author poster at AASTCS 11: Exoplanet Atmospheres (2026).

Observational astrophysics · Fall 2025

Table Mountain sky brightness

I designed a month-long observing plan for the Pomona 1-meter telescope and reduced the images to calibrated sky brightness in three directions. The sky tracks the Moon night to night, and the field toward Los Angeles is about 0.8 mag/arcsec² brighter than the zenith.

Optics lab · 2026

Building and calibrating spectrometers

I aligned a Michelson-interferometer Fourier transform spectrometer that resolved the sodium doublet, and calibrated a scanning grating spectrometer against 11 mercury lines to an average error of 0.014%.

Electronics lab · Fall 2026

AM radio receiver

More research and projects

Exoplanet and Venus atmospheres at MIT, a peer-reviewed algorithm paper, and hands-on work from telescope data to underwater robots.

  • Magma ocean atmospheres

    Coupling LavAtmos, FastChem, and petitRADTRANS to connect magma ocean interiors to observable spectra. Contributing author on a JWST Cycle 6 proposal.

  • Venus UV experiment

    Designed a UV phase-modulation experiment with a lock-in amplifier for in-situ measurement of the Venusian atmosphere.

  • Robust matrix factorization

    Quantile-based update rules for corruption-robust NMF. Peer-reviewed paper at SampTA 2025.

  • Globular cluster distance

    Built a ~150,000-source catalog from archival HST images and fit isochrones to estimate a distance of 12.6 ± 0.6 kpc.

  • Variable star photometry

    Reduced 120 CCD exposures from the 1-meter telescope and measured a δ Scuti pulsation period of 78 ± 5 minutes.

  • Underwater ROV

    Wrote LabVIEW code to read, log, and display live depth data from the ROV's pressure sensor, then deployed it in a lake.
